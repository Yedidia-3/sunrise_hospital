Our Departments

Specialised care units designed to meet every stage of your health journey.

Department

General Medicine Department

Our General Medicine Department is the cornerstone of adult healthcare at Sunrise Care Hospital. Led by board-certified internists, the unit offers comprehensive diagnosis and management for a wide range of conditions — from common illnesses to the long-term management of chronic disease.

Whether you come with a fever, fatigue, or a complex multi-system concern, our physicians take the time to listen and build a personalised care plan tailored specifically to you. Outpatient clinics: Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

Key Services Offered

  • Preventive Health Screenings
  • Chronic Disease Management
  • Diabetes Care & Monitoring
  • Hypertension Treatment
  • Respiratory Illness Care
  • Cardiovascular Risk Assessment
Department

Pediatric Care Department

The Pediatrics Department is dedicated entirely to the health and development of children from birth through 18 years of age. Our team of paediatricians and nurses understands that children require specialised medical attention — not simply scaled-down adult care.

We have created a warm, welcoming, and child-friendly environment that eases anxiety for young patients and their families. Our unit is ranked among the top 3 paediatric facilities in Rwanda, and we are proud of the gentle, age-appropriate care that defines our approach.

Key Services Offered

  • Newborn & Neonatal Care
  • Childhood Vaccination Programs
  • Growth & Development Monitoring
  • Childhood Illness Management
  • Nutritional Counselling
  • Paediatric Surgical Referral

Our Services

End-to-end medical services delivered with excellence, speed, and dignity.

Emergency Care

Our Emergency Department is fully equipped and staffed to handle any medical crisis. We guarantee immediate, life-saving attention around the clock.

  • Open 24 hours, 7 days a week, 365 days a year
  • Trauma & Critical Care Unit
  • Cardiac Emergency Response
  • Paediatric Emergency Bay
  • Burns & Wound Management

Laboratory Services

Our Clinical Laboratory delivers accurate diagnostic results that guide effective treatment. Staffed by qualified medical scientists, we process thousands of samples daily with precision and confidentiality.

  • Haematology & Biochemistry
  • Microbiology & Culture Testing
  • HIV, Hepatitis & STI Screening
  • Blood Glucose & Lipid Profiles
  • Results within 24 hours (routine panels)

Outpatient Services

Our Outpatient Clinic brings specialist care to you without overnight admission. With flexible scheduling and minimal waiting times, quality care fits around your life. Available Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM – 5:30 PM.

  • Specialist Consultations
  • Minor Surgical Procedures
  • Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation
  • Dental & Eye Clinics
  • Chronic Disease Follow-ups

Inpatient Care

Our Inpatient Wards provide round-the-clock monitoring and care for admitted patients. Each room is designed for comfort, privacy, and rapid recovery with nursing support available at all times.

  • Private & General Wards
  • ICU & High-Dependency Unit
  • Post-Operative Recovery Care
  • Dietary & Nutritional Support
  • Structured Family Visiting Hours
